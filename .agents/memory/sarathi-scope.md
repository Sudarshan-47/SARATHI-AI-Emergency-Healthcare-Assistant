---
name: SARATHI feature boundaries
description: Scope constraints for SARATHI AI product redesigns and implementation.
---

Keep the redesigned web and mobile experience within the existing product surface. Preserve existing authentication and profile behavior, APIs, triage and chat, voice input, 108 emergency calls, hospital discovery, and WhatsApp alerts. Do not add a Health Passport or appointments; neither is implemented. Do not invent clinical results or hospital listings. The optional video remains secondary and unfinished.

**Why:** The user explicitly constrained the redesign to implemented flows and real data.

**How to apply:** Before introducing UI or routes, confirm that the flow already exists; redesign it without changing its underlying behavior.
